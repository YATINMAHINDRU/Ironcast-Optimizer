## 🚀 Core Capabilities: The Engine Room Suite

### 🎛️ Real-Time Telemetry Visualizer
Transforms the game's textual and numeric output into a dynamic, animated schematic. See heat dissipation as a flowing thermal gradient, energy routing as a luminous pipeline, and hull integrity as a structural stress map. This responsive UI adapts to your resolution and HUD scale, ensuring an unobtrusive yet omnipresent view of your machine's health.

### 🧠 Predictive Resource Scheduler
An intelligent assistant that forecasts your energy & ammo reserves two turns ahead. It anticipates weapon cooldowns, capacitor recharge cycles, and module heat accumulation, painting a predictive curve so you can plan aggressive strikes or defensive postures without hesitation. This feature is the difference between reacting to chaos and authoring the narrative of battle.

### 🗺️ Tactical Opportunity Mapper
A non-intrusive overlay that highlights enemy vulnerability windows based on their own heat signatures and cooldown patterns. This isn't a cheat; it's an advanced analysis of publicly visible combat data, presented with crystal-clear annotation. The Mapper respects the integrity of the game, offering suggestions while leaving every final decision firmly in your hands.

### 🛠️ Community-Tuned Preset Library
A repository of operator-created configuration presets. Whether you prefer a minimalist aesthetic with only critical alerts, or a comprehensive command center with every gauge exposed, the library offers a spectrum of styles. The integrated preset editor allows deep customization, and its multilingual support interface ensures operators worldwide can share their optimal layouts.

## ✨ Why SteelPilot Stands Apart

- **Non-Intrusive Design:** Runs as a borderless, click-through overlay. It observes the game state without injecting any code into the game's process, ensuring absolute stability (no memory editing, no runtime patching). It achieves its magic through pure, passive input capture and state prediction.
- **Zero-Signal-Interference Architecture:** The companion operates on a separate visual layer. Your game's anti-cheat and online service integrations remain entirely untouched, operating as if the companion wasn't there. This is a "think-with-you" tool, not an "act-for-you" utility.
- **Adaptive Learning Curve:** With three operator modes (*Cadet*, *Veteran*, *Legend*), the informational density scales from simplified icons to comprehensive engineering diagrams. Your companion grows with your skill, never overwhelming you with raw data early on, yet always ready to feed your hunger for granular detail.
- **24/7 Operator Support & Community Hub:** While the companion runs offline, our dedicated support team is available around the clock via the community Discord and GitHub issue tracker. We treat every query like a critical system failure—swift, precise, and with a complete diagnostic log.
- **Beautifully Responsive UI:** Crafted with a modular design language. Resize, dock, or minimize individual panels. The UI remembers your layout preferences per machine type, so switching from a fast scout to a heavy siege frame automatically reconfigures your control panel.

## 📖 Frequently Asked Troubleshooting

- **Q: The overlay is not appearing.**
    - **A:** Ensure the game is running in **Windowed** or **Borderless Windowed** mode. Exclusive Fullscreen often hides external overlays. Check your system's security software permissions.
- **Q: The game updated, and now the companion doesn't detect it.**
    - **A:** We typically release a compatibility patch within 24-48 hours of a major game update. Please check the [Releases] section for a newer version. The companion scans for UI signatures, which can change with patches, so staying current is key.
- **Q: Is my data sent anywhere?**
    - **A:** No. All analysis happens on your local machine at the edge of the processing pipeline. The only network calls are update checks performed when you click "Check for Updates" manually. Your telemetry is yours alone.
